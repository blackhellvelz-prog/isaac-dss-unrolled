# Credits

Other mods this mod works with. Their files are not changed and not included here: our code reads their data or wraps their functions at runtime, and does nothing when the mod is not installed.

| Mod | Author | What our code does | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Encyclopedia - An In-Game Wiki!](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2376005362) | AgentCucco | the Dead Sea Scrolls core shipped in it (and in other mods) is redrawn at runtime: panels, icons, title and tip | `dss_scroll.lua` |
| [Mod Translator](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2881743468) | Goganidze | its Cyrillic DSS font (menu_rfont) is used for Russian text | `dss_scroll.lua` |
| [Mod Config Menu Pure](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3680349610) | t33ot | sections of Mod Config Menu are opened from the shared mods window | `dss_scroll.lua` |
| [Passive Skill Trees (Rep+)](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3305869734) | Ab Aeterno | its skill tree is opened from the shared mods window | `dss_scroll.lua` |

Library: [REPENTOGON](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3127536138) by jerb and the REPENTOGON team — required.

Thanks to the authors of these mods and to the whole Isaac modding community.
